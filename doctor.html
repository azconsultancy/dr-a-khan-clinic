Dr. A. Khan sees patients for skin, hair and scalp concerns at his Civil Lines clinic. Consultations also cover laser and obesity care, so you can choose the type of visit that matches your concern.

You’ll find the clinic on the first floor of Shanshaah Building, Sonal Tower, opposite Axis Bank. To arrange a first consultation or follow-up, send your preferred date and time on WhatsApp; the clinic replies to confirm availability and the consultation fee.
